How it began

Our Story

Every great love story has a beginning. Ours just happened to start with a disagreement!

We first met in Kericho at work, and to say we hit it off immediately would be a complete lie. In fact, our first encounter went so badly that I somehow earned a permanent spot on her "Do Not Like" list. For months, I was practically the villain in her story.

But life had other plans.

One day, an unexpected opportunity came up for me to visit her, and that visit changed everything. The "enemy" status slowly disappeared, and before we knew it, we had become friends. Then came the endless conversations — talking day and night until our SMS bundles surrendered and gave up on us!

As we each continued with our own lives — working, going out, meeting people, and chasing our dreams — our friendship only grew stronger. Somewhere along the way, friendship became best friendship, and best friendship quietly turned into something much more.

Before we knew it, feelings had taken over, and love had found its way into our hearts.

"From strangers, to friends, to soulmates — it's amazing how a story that began with a misunderstanding turned into the greatest blessing of our lives."

After two wonderful years of friendship, we decided in 2023 to take the next step and officially start dating. What followed was a beautiful journey filled with laughter, adventures, lessons, ups, downs, and countless memories.

Then came the day that changed everything — 14th May 2026. A question was asked, a heart skipped a beat, and thankfully, the answer was a very enthusiastic "YES!" 💍✨

To everyone who walked with us through our dating journey, thank you for your love, support, and encouragement. And now, as we begin this exciting new chapter, we warmly welcome you to celebrate the next step with us.

With all our love, Ray & His Forever Favourite Person ❤️
